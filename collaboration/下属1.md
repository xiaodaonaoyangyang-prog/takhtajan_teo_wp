## Statement defect (Hard rule 1): `lemma_8_4` is nearly vacuous

`∃ δ > 0, (∀ z ∈ unitDisc, ‖κ z‖ < δ) → Tendsto …` is quantified *after* κ is fixed. For κ ≢ 0 take
δ = ½·sup‖κ‖ > 0: the hypothesis is false, the implication is trivially true. Only κ ≡ 0 carries content (and then
w_κ = id and it reduces to Lemma 8.2). The book's "‖κ‖∞ small enough" means a δ uniform in κ.
